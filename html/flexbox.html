What to point out

  • Flexbox has two axes. flex-direction decides which one is the main axis, and everything else follows from that.
  • justify-content works along the main axis. align-items works across it. Swap the direction to column and the two trade places.
  • align-items: stretch is the default, which is why boxes fill the container height until you change it.
  • flex-grow shares out leftover space. flex-shrink decides who gives way when there is not enough.
  • order moves a box visually without touching the HTML. Handy, but screen readers still follow the source order.

Try this with the class

  • Set justify-content: space-between, then switch direction to column. Ask why the boxes now spread vertically.
  • Give box 3 a flex-grow of 1 and ask where the extra space went.
  • Set every box's flex-basis to 200px with nowrap, then switch to wrap.
  • Give box 5 an order of -1 and ask what moved — the box, or the HTML?
